# Source and license notices: Craft App Suite media

These unmodified assets represent the **individual Craft Apps**, not the ArtCraft company or launcher identity. They do NOT include the ArtCraft wordmark, the ArtCraft A trademark or launcher brand imagery.

13 product icons originated from the individual Craft Apps and are redistributed under the app-specific MIT OR Apache-2.0 licenses, as indexed in [ArtCraft Launcher attribution](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher/blob/9419d89/ATTRIBUTION.md).
39 screenshot JPGs are pinned to [craft-launcher-assets v1](https://github.com/storytold/craft-launcher-assets/blob/v1/ATTRIBUTION.md); the authors, their source images and artwork licenses are recorded individually in that attribution.
4 extra screenshots are from [PdfCraft](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft/tree/0dc77fe4ed8810b9e9f25b9dd050afae662c062a/docs/images), and 4 from [FigCraft](https://github.com/storytold/figcraft/tree/80f916326fb94f3a88cb24e64f396f564a0ebc06/docs/images), under Apache-2.0 for their code and UI.

User-visible images are authentic project interface screenshots, not illustrative mockups. They are displayed with links to their exact pinned source files.
